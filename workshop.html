Frequently Asked Questions ❓

Everything you need to know before joining.

Is this workshop free?

Yes. There is no registration fee.

Who can attend?

Students from any country can join who are applying for GKS.

How can I register?

Simply click the Register Now button and complete the registration form.

Will the workshop be recorded?

The recording may not be available. We encourage all participants to attend the live session.

Will this workshop guarantee a scholarship?

No. The workshop provides guidance, strategies, and practical information to help you prepare a stronger application, but scholarship selection depends on the official selection process.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. E-certificates will be provided to participants who meet the attendance requirements.

What should I prepare before joining?

Keep a notebook, pen, and any scholarship-related questions you'd like to ask during the Q&A session.

How can I stay updated about future workshops?

You can follow The Global Path on social media and join our official WhatsApp Community to receive updates about upcoming workshops and scholarship opportunities.

Can I ask questions about my own scholarship profile?

Yes. During the Q&A session, you can ask general scholarship-related questions. For personalized guidance, you may book a one-on-one consultation with me.
