GKS FAQ

Everything you need to know before you apply.

Is TOPIK mandatory for GKS?

No. TOPIK is not mandatory for most applicants, but having a TOPIK certificate can strengthen your application and may provide additional benefits.

Can I apply if I have a gap year?

Yes. A gap year does not automatically disqualify you. You should be able to explain how you used your time productively.

Can I apply through both the Embassy Track and University Track for GKS?

No. You can only submit one application in a single GKS cycle.

How can I write an effective Personal Statement?

Focus on your story, not just your achievements. Clearly explain your motivation, academic journey, career goals, and why you're a strong fit for the GKS program. Keep it authentic, well-structured, and tailored to your chosen field of study.

What are the most important documents?
  • Application Form
  • Personal Statement
  • Study Plan
  • Recommendation Letters
  • Academic Transcripts & Certificates
  • Proof of Citizenship
  • Other supporting documents (if applicable)
Can you guarantee my selection?

No. The final selection is made by the GKS selection committee, so no one can guarantee admission. What I do guarantee is personalized guidance to strengthen your profile, prepare your documents according to GKS requirements, and help you submit the best application possible so you can compete confidently with other applicants.

Do you provide complete application guidance?

Yes. I offer consultation, profile assessment, SOP and Study Plan guidance, CV review, document review, and end-to-end application support for all fully funded scholarships based on the service you choose.

Can I write my own Letter of Recommendation (LOR)?

Ideally, no. A Letter of Recommendation should reflect your recommender's genuine opinion about your academic abilities, character, and potential.

However, many professors may ask you to prepare a draft because they are busy. In that case, you can write a draft based on your actual achievements and experiences, and your recommender should review it, make any necessary changes, approve it, and sign it.

How can I book a consultation session?

Click the "Book a Service" button, fill out the booking form with your details, select your required service, and submit it. After reviewing your request, I'll contact you to confirm your session and share the payment and meeting details.

Can international students book your services?

Yes. My services are available to students from all countries through online consultations.

Can I apply if my degree is not yet completed?

Yes, if you are expected to graduate before the deadline specified in the GKS guidelines and can provide the required supporting documents.
